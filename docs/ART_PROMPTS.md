# Project: Baja — ART_PROMPTS (for ChatGPT image generation)

Last updated: November 21, 2026. Paste one prompt per request. All of these are original, unbranded art: no real logos, badges or makes. Never write "Volkswagen", "VW", "Toyota" or any real brand on anything. Real **place names** (cities, towns, highways) are fine; real company names and logos are not.

**Shared style line.** Add it to the end of any prompt if the results drift:
> Grounded semi-realism, warm late-afternoon desert light, dusty air, sun-faded colours, no heavy bloom, no post-apocalyptic look. Palette: sand #C4A574, clay #A46A4B, sage #778571, graphite #353B40, cream #E5DDD0, orange #DC652D.

**Story in one line (for context):** a young adult, fresh out of college, inherits grandpa Walt's run-down garage in the middle of the desert (Baja County) and grandma's old rounded Bug-style car sitting dead inside. Grandpa's old #7 desert race car sits under a tarp in the side yard. A neighbour, Dusty, who runs the gas station, gives them his old El Cajon coupe-utility as a rusted shell to rebuild.

## How we work: groups (same as the build)

We build in **groups**. Work through one group at a time, and **do not start the next group until Chris says "go"**. Each group below matches a group of game work, so art arrives when the build needs it.

| Group | Game work | Art status |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Roadside and finds** | rest areas, gas stops, searchable boxes and crates, wrecks at the roadside | built: art is **reference to restyle what exists** |
| **B. Vehicles and controls** | El Cajon (rusted and restored), headlights at night, tire racks in the bed, the Tab inventory | built: art is reference and UI look |
| **C. Ordering and the shop** | phone Internet app (Parts, Tires, Rims, Supplies, Tools), the shop pickup area, supply icons | built: art is UI look and icons |
| **D. World look** | the coast on the real map (LA to Cabo), terrain character per region, desert dressing props | **next to build: needs your art first** |
| **E. Cities** | real-named towns and cities, their layout and landmark buildings | **after D: needs your art and layout first** |

Groups A to C are already in the game, so for those we want pictures that show how they *should* look; Claude will restyle to match. Groups D and E are not built yet: your pictures and layouts decide how they look, and Claude does the data, placement and mechanics (including using assets that already exist in the repo).

**What to send back with each image** (so Claude can use it without guessing): a file name in the form `group-letter_subject_version.png` (for example `D_ensenada-bay_v1.png`), 2048 px or larger, a transparent background for anything that is an object or icon, and one line saying what it shows. Put files in `assets/art/<group letter>/`.
